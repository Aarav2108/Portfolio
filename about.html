About Me

A short professional biography: who I am, why I study IT and where I am heading.

Why I Chose Information Technology

I have always been curious about how technology works behind the screen. What started as fixing family computers and experimenting with simple web pages grew into a real interest in how software is designed and built. I chose Information Technology because it combines problem-solving with creativity, and because it offers the chance to build tools that make a practical difference to people and businesses.

Career Goals

In the short term, I aim to complete my qualification and gain workplace experience through an internship or graduate programme. In the long term, I want to work as a software developer on a team that builds reliable, well-designed web applications, and to keep growing towards a senior or specialist role.

Interests Within IT

I am most interested in front-end and full-stack web development, database design and cybersecurity. I also enjoy learning how cloud platforms are used to deploy and scale modern applications.
