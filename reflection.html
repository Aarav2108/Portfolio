Professional Reflection

Looking back on this module and forward to the next steps in my development.

What I Have Learned

This module showed me that being an IT professional is about much more than writing code. By studying the Move With Me research project, in which a team designed a wearable cape to encourage physical activity in rural South African children, I learned how different IT roles, such as business analysts, solutions architects, project managers and UI/UX developers, work together on a real project. I also learned why professional bodies such as the IEEE matter, and how principles like protecting public safety and privacy and treating people fairly should guide technical decisions. Most importantly, I learned that the digital divide is not only about access to devices and internet connectivity, but also about digital skills and involving communities in designing the technology they will use.

My Strongest Skill

My strongest skill at the moment is communication, especially researching a topic and explaining technical ideas clearly in writing. This module required me to analyse an academic paper, connect it to the module content and present my arguments in a structured way. The Move With Me project also showed me why this skill matters in practice: the researchers only understood problems such as short battery life and unreliable electricity because they listened to and communicated clearly with the people who would use the technology.

The Skill I Would Like to Improve

The skill I would most like to improve is practical software development, particularly building Android applications with Kotlin. The project I studied used a Kotlin app that received movement data from an ESP32 microcontroller over Bluetooth Low Energy and worked offline. I understand how the system fits together in theory, but I want the hands-on ability to build something similar myself.

How I Will Develop That Skill

I will develop this skill by building small, focused projects, starting with a simple Android app and then connecting it to an ESP32 board or an accelerometer to detect movement. I will practise programming problems regularly on platforms such as LeetCode and HackerRank to prepare for technical assessments, and I will upload my work to GitHub so that my progress is visible and well documented.

How This Portfolio Supports My Future

This portfolio, together with my GitHub and LinkedIn profiles, gives me a professional online presence, which the module recommends because recruiters actively search for candidates online. Instead of only listing skills on a CV, I can show real evidence of what I can do. As I complete more modules and projects, I will keep adding to it, so it grows with me and supports both my future studies and my career in the ICT sector.
